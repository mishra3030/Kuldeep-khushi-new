A Letter For You

A few words, filled with love and countless feelings.

Meri Pyaari Khushi ❤️

Dear Khushi,

Dekhte hi dekhte humari shaadi ko ek saal ho gaya. Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki waqt kitni jaldi beet jaata hai. 24 November 2025 ko shuru hua humara yeh khoobsurat safar aaj apna pehla saal poora kar chuka hai.

Yeh ek saal mere liye bahut khaas raha. Is dauraan humne kitni saari chhoti-badi yaadein banayi, kitne khoobsurat pal saath bitaye aur zindagi ke naye-naye rang dekhe. Tumhara saath hona meri zindagi ko aur bhi khoobsurat banata hai.

Main shayad har baar apni feelings shabdon mein bayaan na kar paun, lekin itna zaroor kehna chahta hoon ki tum mere liye bahut special ho. Tumhari muskurahat, tumhara saath aur tumhara meri zindagi ka hissa hona mere liye kisi tohfe se kam nahi hai.

Bas yahi chahta hoon ki humara yeh saath hamesha yunhi bana rahe. Zindagi mein chahe kitne bhi utaar-chadhaav aayein, hum ek-doosre ka haath thaam kar har pal ko khoobsurat banate rahein.

Aaj humari first anniversary par tumse bas ek hi baat kehni hai — yeh pehla saal toh bas ek shuruaat hai, abhi toh humari zindagi ke bahut saare khoobsurat panne likhne baaki hain.

Thank you meri zindagi ko itna khoobsurat banane ke liye.

Happy First Anniversary, Khushi!

Dher saara pyaar,
Tumhara Kuldeep ❤️
